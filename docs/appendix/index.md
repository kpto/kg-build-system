## In this section

The book carries the argument. The appendices carry the exact material: names,
signatures, requirement tables, document shapes, refusal messages and measured
evidence. They are written for lookup, not for reading from front to back.

Each appendix also repeats a rule the book states in context. That repetition is
deliberate: the chapter explains why the rule exists, and the appendix states the
rule precisely enough to check a change.
